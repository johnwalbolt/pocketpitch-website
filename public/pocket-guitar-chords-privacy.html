Privacy Policy

Pocket Guitar Chords · Last updated: June 16, 2026

The short version: Pocket Guitar Chords does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Guitar Chords (“the app,” “we,” “us”) handles your information. We have designed the app to function without gathering any data about you.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Pocket Guitar Chords does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other personal details. The app does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

Any preferences or settings you choose are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Information Collected by Apple

When you download or use an app from the App Store, Apple may collect certain information, such as download statistics, crash data, or device information, in accordance with Apple’s own privacy practices. This collection is performed by Apple, not by us, and is outside our control. You can review how Apple handles this information in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the app collects no data at all, no information from children is ever gathered.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal information, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete, or export. If you have questions about privacy regulations such as the GDPR or CCPA as they relate to this app, you may contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
